1. Who we are

This app is operated by Cyberclick Agency S.L. ("Cyberclick", "we", "us"), registered in Barcelona, Spain. For privacy questions write to privacy@cyberclick.net.

2. What this app does

The Cyberclick HubSpot Audit connects to a HubSpot portal via OAuth in order to:

  • Read account-level information about the portal (ID, name, currency, timezone, domain).
  • Count records in standard CRM objects (contacts, companies, deals, tickets) and read deal pipeline status.
  • Generate a portal snapshot visible to the installer at our public dashboard.
  • Notify the installer by email and pass the snapshot to Cyberclick's team for follow-up.

3. Data we collect from your HubSpot portal

  • Portal identifier (Hub ID), portal display name, configuration metadata.
  • Aggregate counts of standard CRM objects.
  • Deal pipeline definitions and aggregate deal status counts.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by HubSpot, stored encrypted at rest.
  • The email address of the user who installed the app (the "installer").

We do not read or store individual contact records, conversations, files, or any personally identifiable information about your CRM contacts beyond the aggregate counts described above.

4. How we use your data

  • To generate and deliver your portal snapshot.
  • To allow Cyberclick's team to prepare a personalized audit and reach out.
  • To send the installer one email with a private link to the snapshot.
  • To improve the reliability and performance of this service.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising. Internally, your snapshot is accessible only to authorized Cyberclick staff who deliver the audit follow-up. We use trusted infrastructure providers (HubSpot, Amazon Web Services, our hosting provider) under appropriate data-processing terms.

6. Retention

We retain your portal data for as long as the app remains installed and for a limited period after uninstall to honor any pending follow-up. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@cyberclick.net. Uninstalling the app from HubSpot revokes the access tokens immediately.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights write to privacy@cyberclick.net.

8. Security

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest. Connections are secured with TLS. We restrict access to portal data to the minimum staff required to deliver the audit follow-up.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision. Material changes will be notified to active installations.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related question, write to privacy@cyberclick.net.
